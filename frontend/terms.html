1. What the service is

NexAPI is a reseller gateway. We hold access to one or more third-party APIs and sell you a key that lets you call them through us. When your key hits the gateway, we check it, forward the request upstream with our own credentials, and hand you back the answer.

We are a middle layer, not the origin of the data. The underlying API is operated by someone else. Some plans call more than one upstream and merge the responses into a single reply.

  • Keep it secret. A key is a bearer credential — whoever holds it can spend your requests. We cannot tell them apart from you.

  • You may not resell, sublicense, or share your key. Every buyer needs their own.
  • If your key leaks, tell us and we will disable it. A replacement for the remaining time is at our discretion; leaking your own key is not grounds for a refund.
  • Retrieve your keys any time from the dashboard using your email.

6. Plan duration and expiry

Each plan runs for a fixed period that starts the moment the key is issued — not when you first use it. A Lifetime plan has no expiry date; "lifetime" means the lifetime of this service, not a guarantee of perpetual operation.

When the period ends the gateway deactivates the key automatically and further calls are refused. Unused requests do not roll over and are not refunded. Buy a new plan to continue.

7. Request limits and fair use

A plan may carry a request limit. Every call that reaches the upstream counts, whether or not the result is what you wanted — a failed OTP is still a delivered request. Calls we reject before forwarding, such as an invalid or expired key, do not count.

Once the limit is reached, the gateway answers 429 until the plan renews or you buy a new one. Even on an unlimited plan we may throttle or suspend traffic that threatens the stability of the gateway or its upstreams.
