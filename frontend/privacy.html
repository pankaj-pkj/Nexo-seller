Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 August 2026

NexAPI is an API gateway. You buy a plan, we issue you a key, and that key lets you call an upstream API through us. This page says exactly what we keep while doing that, where it lives, and how to get rid of it. We collect the minimum a paid gateway needs to work — nothing is sold, rented, or used for advertising.

1. What we collect

Three things, and only these:

  • Your email address. You type it at checkout. It is the only way to attach a key to you and the only way you can look it up again on the dashboard. We do not ask for a name, address, or phone number.
  • Your API key and its plan. The key we generate, which plan it came from, when it expires, and how many requests it has used.
  • A log line per API call. When your key calls the gateway we record the time, the key, the plan, the HTTP status, how long the upstream took, the IP address the request came from, and the query parameters you sent us to forward.

Note on request parameters. Whatever you put in the request — a phone number in num=, for example — is forwarded to the upstream API and is written into that log line. Do not send us personal data you are not allowed to send to a third-party API.

2. Why we collect it

  • To deliver what you paid for — issue the key, link it to your email, let you retrieve it later.
  • To enforce the plan — expiry dates and request limits cannot work without counting requests.
  • To keep the gateway up — the logs are how we tell an upstream outage from a broken key, and how we spot a key being abused or shared around.

We do not profile you, we do not run analytics or tracking pixels, and there are no advertising scripts anywhere on this site.

3. Payments and crypto

Payments are handled by Heleket, an external crypto payment processor. When you click Pay, you leave this site for theirs.

  • We never see, hold, or store your wallet keys, seed phrase, or wallet balance.
  • We store the order ID, the plan, the amount, the status, and the email you gave us — enough to match a confirmed payment to a key.
  • What Heleket itself collects on their page is covered by their own privacy policy, not this one.
  • Blockchain transactions are public and permanent by design. Anything visible on-chain is outside anyone's control, including ours.

4. Where it is stored

All of it sits in Google Cloud Firestore, in the Firebase project behind this deployment, reachable only by the service account this server holds. The site itself runs on Vercel. Both are third-party hosts, so your data is processed on their infrastructure and may sit on servers outside your country.

5. Who else sees it

We do not sell or rent anything. Data reaches exactly these parties:

  • The upstream API provider — the parameters of each call you make, because forwarding them is the service.
  • Heleket — your email and the order amount, so a payment can be matched to your key.
  • Google Firebase and Vercel — as the hosts holding the database and running the server.
  • A lawful authority, if we are legally required to hand something over.

6. How long we keep it

  • Keys and their plan records stay while the key is live, and afterwards as a record of the purchase.
  • Payment records are kept as proof of the transaction.
  • Usage logs are operational data and are pruned from time to time; they are not meant to be a permanent archive.

An expired key stops working immediately — the gateway deactivates it on the expiry date — but its record is not automatically wiped. Ask us and we will delete it.

7. Cookies and browser storage

This site sets no tracking cookies. The only thing it puts in your browser is your email in localStorage, so the checkout and dashboard forms are pre-filled next time. It never leaves your device except when you submit a form. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google's servers see the request your browser makes for them. Nothing else on the page calls a third party.

8. Your choices

Message us on Telegram and we will, for the email you bought with:

  • tell you what we hold,
  • correct an email you mistyped at checkout,
  • delete your keys, logs, and records.

Deleting your data kills any key attached to it, and a key that is deleted before its plan runs out is not refunded — so ask for deletion when you are finished with the service, not in the middle of a plan.

9. Security

Traffic runs over HTTPS. The upstream API key never leaves the server and is never sent to your browser — hiding it behind your own key is the point of this gateway. The admin panel is behind a secret, and database access is limited to the server's service account.

That said, no service is perfectly secure. Treat your API key like a password: anyone holding it can spend your requests, and we cannot tell them apart from you.

10. Age

NexAPI is a developer tool and is not directed at children. Do not use it if you are under 18, or under the age at which you can enter a contract where you live.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Continuing to use the service after that means you accept the updated version.
